# 📊 Sales Analysis & Tableau Dashboard

This project focuses on analyzing sales data to uncover key business insights such as revenue trends, profit performance, and product-level contributions using **Tableau**. It aims to support data-driven decision-making and optimize sales strategies through interactive visual storytelling.

---

## 🔍 Key Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit trends across regions and time  
- Identify top-performing products and customer segments  
- Evaluate category-wise profitability and growth  
- Build intuitive dashboards for business decision support  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- **Sales Overview** – Total revenue, profit, and quantity sold  
- **Regional Analysis** – Sales and profit performance by location  
- **Product Insights** – Top-selling and most profitable products  
- **Trend Analysis** – Time-based patterns in sales and profit metrics  

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau** – Interactive dashboards and visual analytics  
- **Excel / CSV** – Data preparation and cleaning  
- **Data Analysis** – Aggregation, KPI calculation, and performance insights  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
The final Tableau dashboard provides an interactive platform for exploring key sales metrics, identifying growth opportunities, and supporting strategic business decisions.
